# First Three Minutes Audit

## Goal

A judge should understand the complete promise of VOLT NOMAD before the first
machine-beast core is recovered:

1. one command immediately damages the enemy and earns CHARGE;
2. CHARGE buys permanent power in one of five visible gear trees;
3. manual commands and the always-on AUTO arsenal accelerate each other;
4. each beast has a readable rule and becomes a permanent core after defeat.

The deterministic five-inputs-per-second benchmark clears the onboarding hunt
in about 184.6 seconds. The first three minutes therefore form one complete
learn-build-defeat arc rather than an isolated tutorial.

## Player-facing sequence

| Moment | Player sees | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Continue/New Hunt, language, settings, story log | One primary action starts play |
| Route | Six distinct beasts and the three-hunt short route | Any beast can be selected |
| Encounter | Short skippable exchange and a three-page field manual | Combat starts without hidden systems |
| Commands 1–3 | `FIELD GUIDE 1/3`, projectile, enemy recoil, impact ring, damage and CHARGE numbers | Input has immediate audiovisual causality |
| First 10 CHARGE | `FIELD GUIDE 2/3`; affordable gear cards pulse amber and say `READY TO BUY` | The next useful action is visible without menu hunting |
| First purchase | `FIELD GUIDE 3/3`; purchase route and combat stats update | Earned currency has visibly increased power |
| Rest of hunt | The guide yields to the beast-specific rule meter | The player experiments rather than follows prompts |
| Defeat | PixelLab collapse, victory signal, dialogue and recovered core | The long-term route and inherited synergy are proven |

## Guardrails

- The field guide appears only during the first hunt and never changes balance.
- Holding an attack key never creates hidden clicks.
- Optional story text can be hidden, but tutorial and combat warnings remain.
- All guidance is bilingual and uses the same keyboard, mouse, touch and
  remappable-gamepad actions as the rest of the game.
- Screen shake and flashes remain controlled by accessibility settings.

## Release checks

- Smoke tests assert all three field-guide milestones and the exact first-node
  price.
- The visual pass confirms a protagonist projectile, enemy hit response and
  distinct AUTO projectile at combat scale.
- The benchmark and 1,440-route release audit must remain green; onboarding
  polish must not alter campaign math or save data.
