## Reddit / longer English post

**VOLT NOMAD — a short incremental boss rush where every click becomes part of your automatic arsenal**

I made VOLT NOMAD for AI Browser Game Jam 4 around the theme CHARGE. It began as several prototypes, but after playtesting I rebuilt the core loop around one idea: clicking should immediately damage the enemy and also fund the systems that eventually make clicking secondary.

The game has six order-independent machine beasts, five interconnected gear trees, a short three-hunt route for judges or casual players, and a much longer optional route with additional bosses, story scenes, a true ending, and an artwork archive. A normal ending takes roughly 10–20 minutes depending on build and reading time.

It is free, runs in the browser, and supports English and Japanese:
https://tsgamestudio.itch.io/volt-nomad

I would especially appreciate feedback on three things: when the build first feels powerful, where the next action becomes unclear, and where progression starts to feel repetitive.

AI usage is disclosed on the page. The project used Codex for implementation and production support, PixelLab and OpenAI image generation for reviewed visual candidates, and Suno for music candidates, with human direction, playtesting, selection, and final decisions throughout.
