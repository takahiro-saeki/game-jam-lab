# PROJECT CHARGE — Artwork Archive Expansion

The complete-clear archive expands from four ending memories to fourteen total works.

1. Gearmaw, Iron Borer
2. Vaultback, Thunder Shell
3. Pyre Wyrm, Furnace Serpent
4. Relay Hydra, Three-Head Bus
5. Swarm Matriarch
6. Phase Mantis

9. Arch Singularity, the World Engine
10. Prime Current — three forms remembered as one final confrontation

## Presentation

- Every image is a 1280×720 cinematic illustration.
- The PixelLab encounter sprite is used as the subject-design reference.
- The existing ending illustrations define the rendering language, lighting density, and palette.
- Volt Nomad appears at small scale where useful, preserving the boss as the primary silhouette.
- The archive uses paged thumbnails so fourteen memories remain readable at 1280×720.
