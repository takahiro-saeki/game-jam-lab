## Full description — ready to paste

**The first current beneath the earth has refused to stop.**

PROJECT CHARGE is a compact incremental boss rush about a lone recovery automaton descending into a subterranean world engine. Every manual command deals damage and generates **CHARGE**. Spend it across five interconnected gear trees, evolve your automatic weapons, and inherit the cores of the machine beasts you defeat.

Choose any three of six hunts to reach a fast normal ending. If the build has you hooked, return for the remaining beasts, the second abyssal boss, ARCH SINGULARITY, and the signal waiting below the world engine.

### What makes a run

- Six order-independent mechanical beasts, each with a different combat rule
- Two abyssal bosses, a three-phase world engine, and an optional three-form final confrontation
- Five gear trees with 86 nodes and 317 attainable standard ranks
- Manual damage, PURE CHARGE generation, drones, gatling and rail mutations, stolen-core synergies, and five simultaneous OVERLIMIT systems
- No game-over punishment: the challenge is finding a build that breaks the required damage curve
- Persistent achievements, playtime records, credits, a true-ending cinematic, and a 14-piece artwork archive after complete clear
- Mouse, keyboard, touch, and gamepad support
- Full English and Japanese text

### Theme: CHARGE!

CHARGE is both the game's resource and its central verb. A click is an attack, a stored command, and the first step in an economy that eventually runs faster than the player can click. The deeper interpretation is emotional: the recovered machine cores are not trophies, but memories carrying the protagonist toward one final choice.

## Controls

- **Mouse / touch:** Select, attack, purchase, and navigate
- **Space / controller A:** Manual command
- **Arrow keys / D-pad:** Navigate maps, menus, and skill trees
- **Enter / controller A:** Confirm
- **Esc / controller Back:** Return
- **T:** Open the gear tree when available
- **L:** Switch Japanese / English
- **M:** Toggle music
- Audio, effects, screen shake, flashes, and language can also be changed from **Settings**.
